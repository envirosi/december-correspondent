# The December Correspondent
## ETN Christmas edition · December 2026

**The promise.** Someone with thirty seconds between calls can open a door, get a laugh and leave. Someone with two minutes can add something. Someone having a difficult December can quietly collect a kind letter from a colleague.

### What the team sees
A warm ivory newspaper with a dark green masthead, cranberry door numbers and a little brass pigeonhole handle. Across the top sit UK · Cebu · Sydney in equal type. On a phone the first screen holds today’s headline, “Open today’s door” and “My pigeonhole”. Past editions sit below. Future doors show only their numbers. Keep the paper legible at large text sizes, support keyboard navigation, honour reduced motion and keep sound off until requested.

Shared doors work as finished miniature features. Use the optional prompt beneath the story only on days with a prompt in the CSV. Private doors carry a different daily miniature, with personal post tucked inside. Everyone gets both calendars, including colleagues who skip the gift exchange. Never show attendance, streaks, read receipts or a tally of who joined in.

### Files for the build
- calendar.csv supplies the 24 shared features, optional prompts and 24 private base entries. Text is ready to publish. Day 1 opens Tuesday 1 December. Saturdays and Sundays carry reading only.
- build.md describes screens, access, data, dates and acceptance checks.
- newsroom.md holds copy to paste, the running order and launch checklist.
- team.csv and letters.csv are empty input templates. Populate privately. A real letter belongs to its recipient. Ask separately before reprinting any part of it.

### First build milestone
Use three fictional readers, one per region, and a separate editor. Demonstrate a shared door, a sealed future door and one private letter end to end. Then load all 24 rows, add the gift draw and try the rehearsal in build.md. Collect the real roster and letters alongside development. They are needed for invitations and personalisation, not for starting the build.
